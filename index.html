ANNUAL REQUIREMENT

15pts

Active member status

Earn at least 15 points per school year to be considered an active HOSA member.

Graduation cord checklist

  • Earn 15 points for each year of membership.
  • Be a HOSA member during senior year.
  • Paying dues alone does not qualify you for a cord.
  • Officers may qualify for an automatic cord under chapter rules; senior-year membership is still required.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

How many points do I need to be an active member?

You need at least 15 points per school year to be considered an active member.

Does paying dues automatically make me active?

No. Paying dues alone does not meet the 15-point active-member requirement.

How do I qualify for a graduation cord?

Follow the chapter's yearly point requirements and remain a HOSA member during senior year. Ask an officer to confirm your individual eligibility.

What if my points are missing or incorrect?

Use the tracker as a personal record and contact a chapter officer or sponsor to verify the official total.

Where can I find my event's rules?

Start with the official HOSA competitive event guidelines and check with your event officer for current instructions.
